# Resolution Life flag sculpture

The previous trefoil has been replaced by an original tessellated interpretation of Resolution Life's triangular flag. The reference is the actual [header logo SVG](https://www.resolutionlife.com/media/pfglbg3u/logo.svg) linked from the [official company site](https://www.resolutionlife.com/), inspected directly as a rendered image during authoring.

The symbol is a red right triangle above the blue lowercase i. Its left edge is vertical, its upper edge horizontal, and its lower edge rises to the right-hand point. The reference triangle measures 15.6471 × 7.29508 SVG units; the asset specifies red `#E53E51` and blue `#202945`. These are colors observed in that official asset, not a claimed comprehensive brand standard.

The source preserves the triangle's projected outline and proportions. A shallow curved crest, 0.20-unit solid thickness, blue reverse and narrow blue perimeter reveal are original presentation choices for a rotating 3D sculpture. They are not official three-dimensional brand artwork. The shape has no knot, hole, invented peak, or supporting wordmark text.

A 120-step triangular lattice produces **29,520 triangles and 14,762 vertices**, including front, reverse and closed perimeter. All positions and colors are prepared at build time. The source has one connected orientable component, no boundary, and Euler characteristic two. Tests check actual silhouette coverage, reference aspect ratio, constant enclosed thickness, substantial front-facing red and a blue reverse. The Klein bottle source is unchanged.

No downloaded logo bitmap, SVG, third-party mesh or implementation is shipped; only this independently authored geometry enters the existing compiler. Final lighting and appearance are handled by the shared renderer and its coordinated browser review.
