Surface maps converted to grayscale from NASA imagery.

Earth: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
Blue Marble data courtesy of Reto Stockli (NASA/GSFC) and NASA Earth Observatory.

Moon: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit.
Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera / Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter.
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/

Files: 4-byte little-endian uint16 width and height, followed by 8-bit grayscale pixels.
Gzip files preserve exactly the same payload.
