personal projects

The Shortest Path Through a Curved World

A C++20 implementation of the Heat Method for finding approximate shortest paths across curved triangle meshes with one, two, or three handles. The solver builds discrete differential operators, reuses sparse factorizations, and traces paths through triangle interiors. An interactive Three.js narrative shows heat diffusion and compares the native paths with straight-line distance and edge-constrained Dijkstra routes.

C++20 | Eigen | Three.js

Open project

When Every Agent Finds the Shortcut

A reproducible Python study of how independent learners find inefficient equilibria in Braess’s routing game. Exact analysis identifies equilibria, social optima, and marginal-cost tolls for populations up to 100,000 agents. The interactive essay connects those results to Q-learning, Hedge, and best-response experiments, showing how individually sensible decisions can produce collective congestion.

Python | NumPy | Three.js
