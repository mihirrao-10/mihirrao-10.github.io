Probabilistic Graphical Models

Notes by Mihir Rao

Probabilistic graphical models give a compact way to represent joint distributions using a graph — Bayesian networks for directed dependencies, Markov random fields for undirected ones — and the algorithms that turn those representations into efficient inference and learning. My notes work through the material chapter by chapter, with interactive diagrams for the ideas that read more easily as pictures than as prose. Based on the Stanford CS228 course notes.
