Probabilistic Graphical Models

Notes by Mihir Rao

A graduate course on the language of probabilistic graphical models — directed and undirected representations, exact and approximate inference, learning under full and partial observation, and applications from hidden Markov models to topic models and nonparametric Bayes. Each lecture below is a standalone web page with embedded interactive diagrams.
