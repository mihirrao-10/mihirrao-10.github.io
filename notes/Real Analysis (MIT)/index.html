Real Analysis

Notes by Mihir Rao

Description. Real analysis is the rigorous study of the real numbers and the functions defined on them — set theory and induction, the structure of $\mathbb{R}$, sequences and limits, continuity, differentiation, and integration. My notes follow the course chapter by chapter, with worked proofs and small interactive diagrams for ideas like set operations.
